# Legal information required before public commercial launch

The live legal pages describe Guaranteed Reply and VIP, but they are **not legally approved**. The owner and qualified French/EU counsel must provide or confirm the facts below. Do not substitute guesses or use the existing draft language as approval.

1. **Operator and legal notice:** exact legal operator name, form and registration status; physical address; contact email and telephone; SIREN/SIRET/RCS and VAT number where applicable; publication director; hosting-provider legal particulars.
2. **Consumer redress:** appointed, eligible consumer mediator and its contact/website details; support and complaint route; applicable withdrawal and immediate-performance language for a secured reply and a recurring VIP subscription.
3. **Checkout disclosures:** which party is the seller/trader for each product, total price and taxes, service deadline, cancellation/refund terms, VIP renewal and cancellation wording, and the precise acknowledgements to record before payment.
4. **Privacy:** verified processor list and agreements, processing locations/transfers, exact retention periods per data class, deletion and data-subject request process, breach-response owner, and contact details for privacy requests.
5. **Creator marketplace:** creator professional/trader disclosures, registration and tax obligations, invoicing/VAT treatment, platform reporting duties, payout and negative-balance language, and moderation/appeal obligations.
6. **Final review:** review `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/creator-terms`, `/community-guidelines`, cookie consent, signup/onboarding acceptance, checkout copy, and French/mobile presentation. Record the reviewer, date and approved versions in `FINAL_OWNER_LAUNCH_STEPS.md`.

Until these are supplied and reviewed, legal status remains **Manual verification required** in Admin → Launch readiness. Code checks and Stripe approval cannot replace this review.
