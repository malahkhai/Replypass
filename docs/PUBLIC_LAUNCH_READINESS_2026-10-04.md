## Release rule

The paid public-launch checkmark stays **open** until the migration is applied, all live-money and VIP acceptance cases above are recorded, production operations are observed, the phone journey passes, and the legal/operator review is signed off. Controlled private testing can continue with clear accounting for test versus live activity. This is a hard-gate assessment rather than a guessed readiness percentage.
