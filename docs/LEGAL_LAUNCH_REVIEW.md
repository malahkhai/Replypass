# Legal launch review — 21 September 2026

This is a product/legal readiness review, not a legal opinion. The public Terms, Privacy Policy, Creator Terms and Community Guidelines now describe the implemented ReplyPass product: adult-only accounts; four launch products; authorization, capture and release rules; 15% platform fee and 85% creator share; private media; VIP renewal/cancellation; moderation; analytics consent; providers; account deletion; disputes and refunds.

## Blocking facts the owner must supply

- Registered operator name (or the individual entrepreneur’s full legal name), legal form, registered/postal address, SIREN/SIRET/RCS details, VAT number if applicable, telephone number, and publication director.
- Hosting notice details in the French legal-notice format.
- The appointed, CECMC-listed consumer mediator and its contact details.
- Whether each founding creator is acting as a professional/trader or private individual, plus the public disclosures required for that status.
- Confirmed provider agreements, processing regions, data-transfer safeguards and exact retention schedule.
- The support/refund operational response times and the legal basis/wording for immediate performance and loss of withdrawal rights at each checkout.

Until these are complete and French/EU counsel has reviewed the flow, keep `LAUNCH_MODE=private` and do not represent the pages as legally approved.
